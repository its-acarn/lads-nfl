02 — How it counts

The rules

Three leagues. LadsLadsLads, I’m bringing Flexi back and SuperKhalilFitz. They’re the only leagues all four play in with head-to-head games. Chop his LLL off is a guillotine league, so it has no results to add.

44 games each. LadsLadsLads runs weeks 1–14 and the other two run weeks 1–15. Everyone plays the same schedule length in the same leagues, so nobody gets extra games.

Every game counts. A win in any of the three leagues is a win here. When two of the four meet in a league, one takes the W and the other the L.

Tiebreak: points for. Two managers level on record are split by total points scored across all three leagues.

03 — For the group chat

Three ways the playoffs could work

Nothing’s decided yet. In all three, a playoff score is the combined points of all three of your rosters that week. Sleeper scores every team every week, so it still works if you’re out of a league’s own playoffs. It all finishes in week 17, because in week 18 NFL teams rest their starters.

A

The Bracket

All four go in, seeded by the table. Semis, then a final. Lose once and you’re out.

Week 17

For

  • Everyone already knows how it works
  • Every week is a straight head-to-head

Against

  • 44 games of regular season only earn you a seed
  • A week-16 dud from one of your rosters finishes you
B

The Head Start

No bracket. All four race on total points over weeks 16 and 17, and the regular-season finish gives you a head start.

For

  • The regular season is worth actual points
  • One bad week doesn’t knock you out
  • All four are still alive in week 17

Against

  • No head-to-head drama
  • A runaway week 16 can settle it early
  • The head-start numbers are a guess until we see real scores
C

Final & Wooden Spoon

Only the top two play for the Cup, over a two-week aggregate. The bottom two play off to avoid the Wooden Spoon.
